Macetohuerto is a comprehensive Android app for managing and caring for your urban plants and small gardens. 

🌱 **Plant Management**
• Register plants with detailed information (name, species, watering frequency, light needs)
• Advanced search and filtering system
• Sort by name, species, next watering, or last watering
• View detailed plant information with care actions

🌿 **Advanced Care System**
• Fertilization tracking with types and frequency
• Transplant control with pot size monitoring
• Complete care history with photos and notes
• Multiple activity management with custom actions
• Seasonal watering frequency adjustment
• Different weekday/weekend watering schedules

💧 **Smart Watering**
• Automatic next watering date calculation
• Visual indicators when plants need water
• Quick watering buttons from main list
• Seasonal frequency adjustments

📸 **Photo Gallery**
• Individual photo gallery for each plant
• Capture photos with integrated camera
• Select from device gallery
• Optional descriptions for photos

🔔 **Smart Notifications**
• Daily reminders using WorkManager
• Shows how many plants need watering
• Android 13+ notification permissions support
• Customizable notification time

🏠 **Home Widget**
• Quick access widget for home screen
• Shows watering needs counter
• Auto-updates every 30 minutes
• Clean design with plant emojis

💾 **Robust Data Storage**
• Room database for local storage
• Reactive data flows with real-time updates
• Repository pattern architecture
• Private app storage for photos

Perfect for urban gardeners who want to keep their plants healthy and thriving!
